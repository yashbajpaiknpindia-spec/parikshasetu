# UP UPESSC Assistant Teacher (Super TET) — Prep

Powers preparation for the Uttar Pradesh government teacher recruitment run by the
**Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC)**. The current
priority is the **Assistant Teacher (Super TET)** exam — written exam ~3–4 Dec 2026.

## Golden rules
1. **Facts from `exam-blueprint.md`, not memory.** The pattern (120 Q / 360 marks,
   +3 / −1), the 10 sections and their weights, eligibility and dates live there.
2. **Two separate exams — don't merge them.** Assistant Teacher / Super TET (Basic
   Education, Classes 1–8, written-merit only) vs TGT/PGT (Secondary, 90 subject +
   30 GS; PGT adds a 40-mark interview).
3. **UP-GK is the differentiator.** Districts, rivers, heritage, schemes (ODOP),
   UP polity and current affairs — weight practice here.
4. **Verify everything on `upessc.up.gov.in`.** The section-wise split, exact vacancy
   count (~12,405 Assistant Teacher), negative-marking rate and OMR mode are all
   provisional until confirmed against the official PDF.

## What to do
- **"Make a UP mock / Super TET paper"** → follow the Assistant Teacher blueprint
  (section weights in `exam-blueprint.md`), enforce +3 / −1 and the 2-hour limit,
  and bias UP-GK. Label questions as *modelled on the pattern*, never as PYQs.
- **"Build a study plan"** → use the 8-week crash plan (front-load Language + Maths +
  UP-GK by mark weight; mocks from week 4; taper in week 8).
- **"Explain eligibility / pattern"** → answer from the blueprint and add the
  "verify on upessc.up.gov.in" caveat, and flag the two big 2026 changes:
  written-merit only (academic weightage removed) and newly-introduced negative marking.

## Quality bar for questions
- One unambiguous correct option; plausible distractors.
- UP-GK items must be evergreen and verifiable (avoid volatile "first/latest" trivia
  unless sourced from a government page); current-affairs items carry a valid-until date.
- Bilingual-friendly (Hindi + English), since the exam is bilingual.
