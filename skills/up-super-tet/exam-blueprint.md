# UP UPESSC Assistant Teacher (Super TET) — Exam Blueprint

> **Verification stance.** Compiled from credible portals + news (Sep 2026), NOT the
> official PDF. Treat every figure as provisional and confirm against the official
> notification on **upessc.up.gov.in** / apply portal **apply.upessc.org**. Highest-risk
> items to re-verify: exact vacancy (11,508 vs 12,405), section-wise split, negative-marking
> rate, OMR mode, accepted TET, and all dates.

## 1. What & who
- **Body:** Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC), Prayagraj — the new commission that absorbed UPSESSB/UPPSC teacher recruitment.
- **Umbrella (Sep 2026):** ~15,012 posts = **~12,405 Assistant Teacher (Super TET, Advt. 05/2026)** + ~2,607 PGT (Advt. 06/2026).
- **Priority target:** Assistant Teacher (Primary, Classes 1–5); an Upper-Primary (6–8) variant exists.

## 2. Eligibility (Assistant Teacher)
- Graduation + teacher training (D.El.Ed / BTC / B.El.Ed / RCI special-ed diploma).
- **Valid UPTET or CTET** (Paper-I for 1–5; Paper-II for 6–8) — qualifying, mandatory.
- Age 21–40 as on 1 July 2026. Relaxations: SC/ST/OBC (UP) +5; PwD +15; ex-servicemen +3; Shiksha Mitra to 60.

## 3. Selection
- **Written-merit ONLY** for Assistant Teacher — academic (Class 10/12/graduation) weightage **removed** this cycle. Then document verification → district/post allotment. No interview.
- (PGT differs: written 360 + interview 40 = 400.)

## 4. Written exam pattern (Assistant Teacher, Primary)
- Mode: OMR / offline (verify). Duration: 120 min. **120 questions · 360 marks.**
- Marking: **+3 correct, −1 wrong** (negative marking newly introduced). Bilingual (Hindi + English).

| Section | Q | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| General Knowledge & Current Affairs (incl. UP) | 25 | 75 |
| Language — Hindi, Sanskrit & English (grammar) | 30 | 90 |
| Mathematics | 16 | 48 |
| Science | 8 | 24 |
| Environmental & Social Studies | 8 | 24 |
| Teaching Skills / Pedagogy | 8 | 24 |
| Child Psychology / Development | 8 | 24 |
| Logical Reasoning | 5 | 15 |
| Information Technology (ICT) | 4 | 12 |
| Life Skills, Management & Attitude | 8 | 24 |
| **Total** | **120** | **360** |

> **[VERIFY]** Section-wise split is portal-reported; the official PDF may differ.
> Upper-Primary variant = 30 GK + 90 subject specialisation.
> TGT/PGT (Secondary) = 90 subject + 30 General Studies; PGT adds a 40-mark interview;
> TGT/PGT negative-marking rate is disputed (−1 vs 1/3) — confirm.

## 5. Timeline (as announced, verify)
- Notification 15 Sep 2026 · Apply 16 Sep – ~15 Oct 2026 · **Written exam 3–4 Dec 2026** · Admit card ~10 days before.
- Fees: Gen/OBC/EWS ₹1,000; SC/ST ₹500; PwD ₹300.

## 6. UP-specific GK (the differentiator)
75 districts / 18 divisions; capital Lucknow; 8 NCR districts. Rivers: Ganga (enters Bijnor,
exits Ballia), Yamuna, Gomti, Ghaghra, Betwa, Ken. Dudhwa/Pilibhit tiger reserves. Kathak
(Lucknow gharana), Chikankari (Lucknow), Banarasi silk. Heritage: Taj Mahal, Fatehpur Sikri,
Sarnath, Varanasi, Ayodhya, Prayagraj. Schemes: One District One Product (ODOP). Polity:
bicameral (Vidhan Sabha + Vidhan Parishad). 1857 revolt centres: Meerut, Kanpur, Lucknow, Jhansi.

## 7. 8-week crash plan (synthesised strategy)
1–2: Language grammar + Maths basics; daily UP-GK; 1 diagnostic mock. 3–4: Pedagogy, Child
Psychology, Science, EVS; daily reasoning; 1 mock/week. 5–6: UP-GK deep dive + ICT + Life Skills
+ weak-area repair; 3 mocks. 7: exam-condition mocks every 2 days with negative marking; OMR
discipline; last 5 years' papers. 8: taper, revise error log + 6 months current affairs, 1 confidence mock.
